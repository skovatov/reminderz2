Reminderz Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Reminderz is a personal automated SMS reminder service used to send user-requested reminders for scheduled tasks and events.

Information Collected

Reminderz uses the mobile phone number voluntarily configured by the user for the purpose of delivering SMS reminder messages.

How Information Is Used

Mobile information is used solely to deliver automated reminder messages requested or configured by the user.

Mobile Information Sharing

Mobile phone numbers, SMS consent information, and opt-in data will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

SMS Messaging

Message frequency varies based on reminders scheduled by the user. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of SMS messages or HELP for help.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information associated with the Reminderz service.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page.
